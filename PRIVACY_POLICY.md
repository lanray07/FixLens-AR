# FixLens AR Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

FixLens AR is a safety-first home maintenance guidance app. The app is designed to work with local mock data by default and does not require account sign-in.

## Data The App Handles

FixLens AR may store appliance profiles, maintenance tasks, scan results, voice transcripts, error-code notes, reports, and subscription status on your device so the app can show your maintenance history and reminders.

Camera, microphone, speech recognition, and notification permissions are used only when you choose the related feature. Camera input supports appliance scanning and AR guidance. Microphone and speech recognition input support voice questions and transcripts. Notifications support maintenance reminders.

## Data Sharing

FixLens AR does not sell personal data. The app does not share your local maintenance records with third-party advertisers.

If you purchase a subscription, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. FixLens AR receives StoreKit entitlement information needed to unlock subscription features, but it does not receive your full payment card details.

## Local Storage And Deletion

Maintenance records are stored on device using Apple's local persistence technologies. You can delete local app records from the Settings screen by choosing Delete all data, or by deleting the app from your device.

## Safety Disclaimer

FixLens AR provides educational maintenance guidance only. It does not replace qualified professionals and does not certify gas, electrical, high-voltage, sealed-system, or safety-critical work.
